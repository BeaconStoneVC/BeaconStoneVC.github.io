Why people movements are counts, not names

BeaconStoneVC records people movements at named-person level from firms' own public pages, but at this beta stage we are not disclosing those details publicly. The site publishes daily totals by region only — no names, no job titles, and no indication of which firm a movement belongs to.

Portfolio-company changes above are about companies, not people, so they are still listed individually. See the methodology for what we hold and how to ask a question about it.
