Methodology

BeaconStoneVC records what venture capital firms and their portfolio companies publish on their own public pages, and the changes between daily checks.

Our standard

A change that isn't correct should never appear here. A change we can't prove is held back and checked again the next day, so a change may appear late or not at all. We would rather be late than wrong.

In November 2026 we discarded everything we had previously published and started again from nothing, because we could no longer tell which of our old records were right. We add firms as fast as we can keep to this standard, so coverage is small and growing.

How a list is checked

  1. Each page is opened in a real browser and saved.
  2. A small program written for that page reads the saved copy and produces a list.
  3. Separately, AI readers go through the saved page piece by piece without seeing that list, and write down what they see.
  4. The two are compared. Any real difference holds that page back, and we keep showing its last proven list and the date it was proven.

How a change is checked

A first check of a page only records where it starts, so it never counts as a change. After that, each change is examined on its own:

  • A company newly listed must be confirmed on the firm's page by two separate AI readers, quoting words that a program then finds on the page.
  • A company no longer listed must be gone from the firm's page on two separate days. We say "no longer listed" because that is what the page proves. It doesn't tell us that an investment ended.

How we test the checks

Every day we plant errors, such as a made-up name on a saved page, a false departure, or the wrong website for a company. They go through the same checks as real data. If any is missed, nothing is published that day.

People

We never publish anyone's name, job title or where they moved to. People changes appear only as daily counts by region, and only where we track at least 10 firms, because a smaller count could point to one firm. They also need separate evidence before they count, so this section may be empty for some time.

Dates

Dates show when we detected a change, not when anything happened in the real world.

Companies and websites

When two firms list a company under different names, we treat them as one company only if two AI readers each find so independently and a program confirms the quoted evidence. Otherwise we keep them apart. A website link appears only after an AI reader has opened the site and confirmed it belongs to that company.

Limits

This is a record of what public pages say, and those pages can themselves be wrong or out of date. These checks make errors rare but can't rule them out. Provided as is, with no warranty. Not investment advice. Please verify against primary sources before relying on anything here.
